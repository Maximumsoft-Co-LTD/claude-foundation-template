# MOC — Map of Content

A **Map of Content** is a curated index note that organizes related atomic notes around a single topic. In Obsidian-style PKM, MOCs act as the "table of contents" for a knowledge domain.

**Why MOCs matter for Claude:** Instead of following links note-by-note (zettelkasten style), Claude opens a MOC and immediately sees the full landscape of a topic — every concept, decision, and pattern in one place. This dramatically reduces context loading time.
