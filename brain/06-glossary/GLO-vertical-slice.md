# Vertical Slice

A task that delivers a **complete, user-visible outcome** spanning both frontend and backend, independently deployable and end-to-end testable.

**Contrast with:** Horizontal slice (layer-based decomposition)

**Example:** "User can log in and see their dashboard" is a vertical slice. "Build login API" is a horizontal slice.
