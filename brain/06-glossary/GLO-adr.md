# ADR — Architectural Decision Record

A document that captures a **significant architectural or team decision** with its context, rationale, and consequences. ADRs are never deleted — only superseded by newer decisions.

**Format used in this project:**
- Status: `active` | `superseded by [[DEC-XXX]]` | `proposed`
- Context → Decision → Rationale → Consequences

**Stored in:** `brain/02-decisions/DEC-NNN-slug.md`

**Registry:** [[../00-MOC/MOC-Decisions]]

**Inspired by:** Michael Nygard's ADR format (Architecture Decision Records)
