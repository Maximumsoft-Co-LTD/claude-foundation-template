# Story Points

A relative, dimensionless unit of estimation representing the effort, complexity, and uncertainty of a user story — **not hours or days**.

**Scale:** Fibonacci sequence: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 (13 = must split before sprint)

**Key rule:** If a story estimates at 13 points, break it down before any sprint planning begins.

| Points | Effort | Typical scope |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| 1-2 | Trivial/Small | Single change, no unknowns |
| 3-5 | Medium/Large | Multi-layer, some unknowns |
| 8 | Very large | Consider splitting |
| 13+ | Too large | **Must split** |

**Contrast with:** Hours-based estimation (unreliable due to individual variation and interruptions)
